Everything the business runs on, in one place

Most wholesale distributors track inventory in one spreadsheet, orders in another, and client balances in a notebook or somebody's memory. DistroHub replaces all three with a single dashboard.

inventory_2

Stock counts that match the shelf

Every order and delivery updates inventory automatically. When five cases go out, the count drops right away, so nobody is guessing what's actually left in the warehouse. Low stock items get flagged before they turn into a missed order.

local_shipping

A record of what went where

Log an order once and follow it from placed to delivered. Quantities, arrival dates, and status are all visible at a glance, so nothing gets lost between the warehouse and the client's door.

groups

Know what a client owes, without digging

Balances update as orders go out and get paid off. Instead of flipping through old invoices, a quick look at a client's page shows exactly where their account stands right now.
