Built out of a real distribution business's actual problems

DistroHub started as an answer to a specific mess: inventory in one spreadsheet, orders scribbled on paper, and client balances tracked from memory. It's software built to fix that, not a product built to sell.

receipt_long

Why it exists

Wholesale distribution often gets bogged down by fragmented tools. One spreadsheet for stock, another for orders, and paper ledgers for client balances. Numbers drift out of sync fast. DistroHub unifies stock, ordering, and accounts into a single platform that stays in sync.

storefront

Who it's built for

Focused entirely on real-world B2B wholesale workflows. Managing bulk product lines, tracking client payments, and handling order fulfillment without bloated enterprise tools getting in the way.

code

Kept simple, on purpose

No framework, no ORM, no build step. Plain JavaScript and raw SQL, chosen so every part of how the app works is understood, not just wired together.
