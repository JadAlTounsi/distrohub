Built out of a real distribution business's actual problems

DistroHub started as an answer to a specific mess: inventory in one spreadsheet, orders scribbled on paper, and client balances tracked from memory. It's software built to fix that, not a product built to sell.

receipt_long

Why it exists

The business this was built for used to run on a spreadsheet for stock, a different one for orders, and a paper ledger for who owed what. Numbers drifted out of sync constantly, and nobody trusted them by the end of a week. DistroHub exists to put all three in one place that actually matches reality.

storefront

Who it's built for

This isn't software built to sell to anyone who signs up. It's shaped around one wholesale distribution business, its products, and how it actually places orders and manages clients day to day.

code

Kept simple, on purpose

No framework, no ORM, no build step. Plain JavaScript and raw SQL, chosen so every part of how the app works is understood, not just wired together.
